# 📚 CIVIL SERVICE EXAM (CSE-PPT) HIGH-YIELD MASTER REFERENCE SHEET

## 1. NUMERICAL ABILITY

### Ratio & Proportion
- **Direct Ratio**: a : b = c : d  → ad = bc (Cross multiply)
- **Inverse Proportion**: a : b :: 1/x : 1/y → a * x = b * y (Product constant)
- **Partitive Ratios**: Divide Q into parts a:b:c → parts = (a/(a+b+c))*Q, etc.
- **Mental Tricks**: For percentages, use 1% and 10% methods. Simplify ratios by dividing by GCD.

### Percentages & Profit/Loss
- **Base-Rate-Percentage Triangle**: 
  ```
  P = B × R
  B = P ÷ R
  R = P ÷ B
  ```
  *(R in decimal: 20% = 0.2)*
- **Discount**: Discount = Orig. Price × Discount Rate; Sale Price = Orig. Price × (1 − Disc. Rate)
- **Simple Interest**: I = P × r × t; Amount = P(1 + rt)
- **Compound Interest**: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt) ; annual: A = P(1+r)^t
- **Profit/Loss**: Profit% = (Profit/CP)×100; SP = CP×(1 ± %)
- **Successive Discounts**: Net discount = d1 + d2 − (d1×d2)/100 *(order doesn’t matter)*

### Word Problems
- **Work Problems**: 
  1/A + 1/B = 1/T ; for n workers: sum of reciprocals = 1/T
- **Age Problems**: Set up grid: Past|Present|Future. Equation: age difference constant.
- **Mixture Problems**: C1·V1 + C2·V2 = C_f·V_f (C in % as decimal)
- **Distance-Speed-Time**: 
  - D = S × T
  - Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time
  - Two equal distances, speeds a & b: Avg = 2ab/(a+b)
  - Relative speed: same dir. subtract, opposite dir. add

### Number Series & Operations
- **Arithmetic Progression**: aₙ = a₁ + (n−1)d; Sum = n/2 (a₁ + aₙ)
- **Geometric Progression**: aₙ = a₁·rⁿ⁻¹; Sum = a₁(1−rⁿ)/(1−r) (r≠1)
- **Patterns**: Alternating, squares, cubes, Fibonacci, *+2, +4, +8*, etc.
- **PEMDAS**: Parentheses, Exponents, ×/÷ (L→R), +/− (L→R)  *(“Please Excuse My Dear Aunt Sally”)*
- **Fraction Trick**: Cross-cancel before multiplying.

## 2. VERBAL ABILITY

### Grammar & Usage
- **Subject-Verb Agreement**:
  - Indefinite pronouns: *each, everyone, somebody* → singular; *both, few, many* → plural.
  - Collective nouns (jury, team) → singular if acting as unit; plural if individuals.
  - Inverted: “There is/are” → verb agrees with noun after.
  - Neither...nor / Either...or → verb agrees with **nearest** subject.
  - Intervening phrases: ignore words between S and V.
- **Pronoun-Antecedent**: must match in number & gender.
- **Parallel Structure**: list items must have same form (all gerunds, all infinitives).
- **Dangling Modifiers**: modifier must clearly refer to a word; avoid illogical placement.
- **Tense Consistency**: keep tense steady unless time frame changes.

### Vocabulary & Analogy
- **High-Yield Roots**:
  - *bene* = good (benefit), *mal* = bad (malice)
  - *dict* = say (dictate), *spect* = look (inspect)
  - *ambi/amphi* = both, *auto* = self, *bio* = life
- **Analogy Types**: Synonym, Antonym, Cause‑Effect, Part‑Whole, Function, Degree, etc.
- **Example**: Knife : Cut :: Pen : Write (function)

### Paragraph Organization
- **Cohesive Devices**: pronouns, repetition, transitions.
- **Transition Words**:
  - Addition: furthermore, moreover, additionally
  - Contrast: however, nevertheless, on the other hand
  - Cause/Effect: therefore, consequently, as a result
  - Sequence: first, next, finally, subsequently
- **Sentence Ordering**: Chronological, General→Specific, Cause→Effect.

### Reading Comprehension
- **Main Idea**: Usually first/last sentence; look for repeated concept.
- **Tone**: Author’s attitude (critical, supportive, neutral, humorous) – detect via word choice.
- **Inference ≠ Assumption**: Inference = conclusion from text; Assumption = unstated premise.

## 3. GENERAL INFORMATION

### 1987 Philippine Constitution
- **Preamble**: “...imploring the aid of Almighty God...” *(memorize opening)*
- **Bill of Rights (Article III)** – Key Sections:
  - §1: Due process, equal protection
  - §2: Right against unreasonable searches & seizures
  - §3: Privacy of communication
  - §4: Freedom of speech, expression, press
  - §5: Freedom of religion
  - §6: Liberty of abode & travel
  - §7: Right to information
  - §8: Right to form unions
  - §12: Miranda rights, right to counsel
  - §14: No ex post facto law or bill of attainder
  - §19: No cruel/degrading punishment; death penalty limited
  - *(22 sections total – know the gist of each)*
- **Three Branches**:
  - Legislative (Congress: Senate & House) – makes law
  - Executive (President & Cabinet) – implements law
  - Judicial (Supreme Court & lower courts) – interprets law
- **Constitutional Commissions**:
  - CSC (Civil Service Commission)
  - COMELEC (Commission on Elections)
  - COA (Commission on Audit)

### RA 6713 – Code of Conduct & Ethical Standards
- **8 Norms of Conduct** *(Mnemonic: **C**an **P**oliticians **J**ust **P**ractice **R**eal **N**oble **C**haracter, **S**ir?)*
  1. **C**ommitment to public interest
  2. **P**rofessionalism
  3. **J**ustness and sincerity
  4. **P**olitical neutrality
  5. **R**esponsiveness to the public
  6. **N**ationalism and patriotism
  7. **C**ommitment to democracy
  8. **S**imple living
- **5 Duties**: 
  1. Act promptly on letters/requests
  2. Submit annual performance reports
  3. Process documents within reasonable time
  4. Act immediately on public’s personal transactions
  5. Make documents accessible to the public
  *(Mnemonic: **A** **S**trong **P**erson **A**lways **M**oves – Act, Submit, Process, Act, Make)*
- **Prohibited Acts**: Financial interests, nepotism, graft, receiving gifts, etc.
- **SALN** (Statement of Assets, Liabilities & Net Worth): 
  - Filed: upon assumption, on or before April 30 annually, upon separation.

### EO 292 & Civil Service Rules
- **Merit System**: Appointments based on fitness/ability via competitive exam.
- **Appointments**: Permanent, Temporary, Co‑terminus.
- **Leave Privileges**: Vacation, sick, maternity/paternity, study leave.
- **Administrative Sanctions**: Reprimand, suspension, dismissal for misconduct/dishonesty.

### Environmental Laws & Peace/Human Rights
- **RA 9003**: Ecological Solid Waste Management – segregation, recycling.
- **RA 8749**: Clean Air Act – emission standards, ban on incineration.
- **Universal Declaration of Human Rights**: 30 articles (right to life, liberty, security).
- **Philippine Peace Process**: Bangsamoro Organic Law; history with MILF, CPP-NPA-NDF.

## 4. CLERICAL ABILITY (Subprofessional)

### Alphabetizing & Filing Rules
- **Personal Names**: 
  - Index as: Last, First, Middle 
  - Example: *Cruz, Juan P.*
- **Business Names**: 
  - File as written, ignore leading articles (a, an, the).
- **Numeric Filing**: Numbers come before letters; 1, 2, 10, 11...
- **Geographical**: Ordered by location; “Assistant Director, Manila Region” filed under “Manila”.
- **Indexing Units**: Consider each separate word/initial, not prepositions.

### Spelling & Proofreading
- **Commonly Misspelled Civil Service Terms**:
  - *acknowledgment* (no ‘e’ after ‘g’)
  - *bureaucracy* (bureau, not beuro)
  - *calendar* (not calender)
  - *commitment* (one ‘t’)
  - *occasion* (two ‘c’s, one ‘s’)
  - *privilege* (no ‘d’)
  - *separate* (not seperate)
- **Proofreading Marks**:
  - Delete: line through with loop
  - Insert: caret ^ with text above
  - Transpose: wavy line or ‘tr’
  - New paragraph: ¶
  - Close up: two curved lines

> **⏳ EXAM TIME-SAVING RULES**
> - For percentage problems: quickly find 10% by moving decimal, then scale.
> - In ratio questions: cross-multiply without solving all – only the part asked.
> - For grammar: underline subject once, verb twice to spot agreement errors.
> - In paragraph organization: spot the “first” and “last” sentence by looking for pronouns or transition signals.
> - On reading comprehension: read questions first, then scan passage.
> - For clerical filing: break names into units and compare unit‑by‑unit.
