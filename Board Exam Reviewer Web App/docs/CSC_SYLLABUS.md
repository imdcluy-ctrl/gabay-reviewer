# CSC-PPT Professional Level Syllabus Breakdown

Based on the official Civil Service Commission (CSC) guidelines for the Pen and Paper Test (PPT) Professional Level, the 170-item exam is strictly distributed across the following domains with a time limit of 3 hours and 10 minutes (190 minutes).

## I. English and Filipino (Verbal Ability)
*Vocabulary, Grammar and Correct Usage, Paragraph Organization, Reading Comprehension*
**Expected Items**: ~30-40 items

## II. Analytical Ability
*Word Association, Identifying Assumptions and Conclusions, Logic, Data Interpretation*
**Expected Items**: ~40-50 items

## III. Numerical Ability
*Basic Operations, Word Problems, Number Sequences*
**Expected Items**: ~40-50 items

## IV. General Information
*Philippine Constitution, Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees (R.A. 6713), Peace and Human Rights Issues and Concepts, Environment Management and Protection*
**Expected Items**: ~20-30 items

## V. Personal Information (Non-Scored)
*Examinee Descriptive Data*
**Expected Items**: 20 items

*(Note: Passing score is strictly 80.00%)*
